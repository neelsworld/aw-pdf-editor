What it does

The PDF jobs that come up every week.

Everything below runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Organise pages

Drag pages into order, rotate, delete, duplicate, crop, or pull a few out into a new PDF. Merge several files into one, or split one by page ranges, bookmarks or file size. Every step can be undone.

Convert both ways

Turn photos, scans, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files into one PDF. Export a PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images or text when you need to reuse what's in it. Office files use Microsoft Office or LibreOffice if you have one.

Mark up and fill

Highlight, comment, draw, stamp "Reviewed" or "Received" with your name and the date, add text or a logo, and fill in forms. Add a watermark or page numbers to every page.

Make scans searchable

OCR reads the text in scanned pages so you can search, copy and highlight it. The scan itself is left exactly as it was. English, Assamese, Bengali and Hindi are built in.

Redact properly

Black out names, phone numbers, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, or any area you mark. The text and image underneath are removed from the file, not just covered, and the app checks that before it saves.

Fix a line of text

Click a line and correct it, in the same place and style. Best for small corrections like a date or a number. This is still a beta.

Make files smaller

Shrink scans and photo-heavy PDFs for e-mail and upload limits, at one of three strengths. The original is kept.

Do it for a whole folder

Compress, OCR, number or export many PDFs in one go. Results land in a folder you pick and nothing is overwritten.

Signing

When the PDF needs your DSC signature

A picture of a signature isn't a digital signature. When a document has to be signed with your USB DSC token, the Sign with DSC button in AW PDF Editor starts AW Signer with your saved file ready to choose. AW Signer is free for 15 PDFs a month.

Privacy

Your files don't leave your computer

There is no account and no upload. The app can check for updates if you let it; that's the only time it goes online. You can read the source code yourself: AW PDF Editor is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later, and comes with no warranty.
